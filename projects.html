My work

A note on these Projects

Over the past four years, I worked as a UX/UI Designer & Frontend Developer at a digital Drupal agency, contributing to the design, prototyping and development of more than 20 large-scale digital projects. The project studies below showcase a selection of the work that best represents my experience. Each project was delivered in close collaboration with designers, developers, project managers and clients, while the content focuses on my personal contributions—from UX thinking and interactive Figma prototypes to scalable frontend development and component-based design systems. As with any agency environment, every successful product is the result of teamwork. These project studies are intended to highlight my role, decisions and contributions within those collaborative efforts. If you'd like to learn more about a specific project or see additional work, I'd be happy to discuss it.

FFG

Modernizing Austria's national funding platform through accessible design and scalable frontend development.

FFG Austria

Frontend Development · UX/UI · 2025 · Splash awards 2025 winner

Open website

Overview

FFG Austria's website serves as the central information platform for research funding, innovation programs and public services. As part of an agency team, I contributed to the redesign and frontend implementation of the platform, with a strong focus on accessibility, responsive design and creating a maintainable component based interface.

The challenge

The redesign process evolved continuously throughout development. While implementing the new design system, layouts and UI components frequently changed until shortly before launch. This required rapid design iterations, close collaboration with developers and designers, and the ability to translate changing requirements into consistent frontend components without slowing down the overall project.

My contribution

  • UX/UI Design
  • Frontend Development
  • Created fully interactive Figma prototypes
  • Participated in user testing sessions
  • Performance optimization
  • Worked with an external design system
  • Adapted interface patterns throughout the redesign
  • Built reusable Storybook components
  • Mobile-first workflow
  • Developed interfaces with Vite, JavaScript, Tailwind and SCSS
  • Ensured accessibility compliance

Design process

Because the visual direction continued to evolve throughout the project, the design process became highly iterative. Rather than simply implementing finished mockups, I continuously refined interfaces, updated prototypes and aligned frontend components with the latest design decisions. This close connection between design and development helped keep the project consistent despite frequent changes.

Technical highlights

  • Component-based architecture - Using Storybook allowed reusable UI components to remain consistent across the project while making collaboration between designers and developers more efficient.
  • Accessibility - Accessibility was considered from the beginning, including semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast ratios and responsive layouts.
  • Responsive development - Following a mobile-first approach ensured every interface worked across a wide range of devices and screen sizes.
  • Interactive prototypes - Interactive prototypes were created to visualize the user experience and gather feedback early in the design process.

Biggest challenge & solution

The most challenging aspect wasn't the technical implementation, it was adapting to constant design changes while maintaining consistency across the interface. Design updates often arrived close to deadlines, requiring quick decision-making, efficient communication and the ability to translate evolving requirements into production-ready frontend components without compromising quality.

Results

  • Fully responsive implementation
  • Improved accessibility
  • Modernized visual identity
  • Component-based frontend
  • Positive client delivery

What I learned

Working on a large public-sector platform strengthened my ability to balance design quality with technical constraints. I learned how valuable clear communication, reusable components and iterative workflows become when multiple disciplines collaborate on a large-scale product.
