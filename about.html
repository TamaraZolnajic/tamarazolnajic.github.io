My journey

My journey into the digital world was not a straight path, it was built through curiosity, persistence and a passion for creating things. Originally from Serbia, I moved to Vienna at 17 and started building my independence early. While working different jobs, my passion for computers and technology was always present. As a lifelong gamer, I was fascinated by digital worlds and how people interact with technology.

After moving to Austria and improving my German skills, I discovered a Full-Stack Web Development program and received a full scholarship. That opportunity became the beginning of my professional career in tech.

A year later, I joined my first company where I spent four years working on digital products and websites. While my original focus was development, I discovered my passion for UX/UI design and started learning everything from wireframes and prototypes to design systems and user experience.

The combination of design and development became my biggest strength. I understand both sides of the process — creating designs with developers in mind and building interfaces with the user experience as the priority.
